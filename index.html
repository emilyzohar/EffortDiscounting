Instructions

In this task, you’ll make choices about how hard you want to work and earn credits for a raffle.

On each trial, you will:

  1. Choose a difficulty. Pick between an easy or a hard version of the task. Hard tasks are worth more credits in the raffle.
  2. Watch three numbers. After you choose, three numbers will appear one at a time. Try to keep them in mind.
  3. Do the mental addition. You’ll see a value like +1, +3, or +7. Imagine adding that number to each of the three numbers you just saw. (+1: 195 -> 206; 9 loops back to 0)
  4. Pick the correct sequence. Two answer options will appear, each showing a sequence of three numbers. Choose the option that correctly shows the result of the addition for all three numbers.

You’ll first complete a few practice trials so you can get used to the task. Your real data will only be recorded during the main task.
