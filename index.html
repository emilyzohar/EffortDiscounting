  • Once you pick a difficulty, you will see three numbers appear one at a time.
  • Your job is to keep them in mind.
  • Then you’ll see two answer options (each is a sequence of three numbers).
  • Choose which option correctly shows the result of adding the shown number (e.g., +1, +3, +7) to each of the original three numbers.

You’ll first complete a few practice trials so you can get used to the task. Your real data will only be recorded in the main task.
